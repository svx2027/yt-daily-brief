Timing: posted 15:09 UTC. Format: 5m56s, this channel's standard length, but a full genre swap from AI-news reaction to comedic commentary.

**2. Theo/t3.gg — "Even The NSA Got Involved"**
Packaging: a curiosity-gap title naming a government agency without saying why, distinct from his usual topic-naming style ("Opus 5.5 is proof that..."). Hook: per the transcript, opens by rapid-firing a list of same-day incidents in one breath — "PHP poned, Node.js poned, light LLM poned, GitLab... and even railway got railed" — stacking multiple breaking stories before promising an angle he says nobody else is covering. Timing: posted 19:14 UTC, same day as the incidents. Format: 29m23s, longer than his typical ~20-minute upload, driven by the number of distinct stories covered.
