- `breakoutScore` is vidIQ's own proprietary metric, taken as-is — not independently recalculated. It reads `null` on videos still too fresh for vidIQ to score (IBM Technology's newest upload below).
- `vph` (views per hour) from vidIQ reads as a recent trailing-window velocity, not a lifetime average. **Every age below is calculated from the video's actual `publishedAt` timestamp to snapshot time, never from `views ÷ vph`.**
- The **Outlier multiple** column is this series' own calculation: a video's vidIQ `vph` divided by the median `vph` of that channel's 10 immediately preceding tracked uploads (excluding the video itself). It is **not** a vidIQ field.
- All four rows below are flagged **fresh** (10.4–19.5 hours old at snapshot time), so these multiples lean on vph reads still inflated by recency and will likely come down as each video matures, consistent with this series' prior observations.
