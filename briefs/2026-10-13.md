## Data source and limits

All numbers below came from live `vidiq_channel_videos` calls (recent/non-popular uploads, long-form) made during this run, scoped to this series' six-channel watchlist: Fireship, Theo/t3.gg, IBM Technology, AICodeKing, The PrimeTime, Matthew Berman. Snapshot taken 2026-10-13, approximately 03:30 UTC (estimated from the freshest tracked video's views-over-VPH read at pull time; see caveat below).

- `breakoutScore` is vidIQ's own proprietary metric, taken as-is — not independently recalculated. It can drift between calls seconds apart (flagged in prior editions), so treat it as directional.
- `vph` (views per hour) from vidIQ reads as a recent trailing-window velocity, not a lifetime average — confirmed again today: back-deriving a video's age from `views ÷ vph` gave wildly inconsistent results across channels (Theo's video implied ~47.7h old by that math, but its actual `publishedAt` timestamp puts it at ~24.9h old). **Every age below is calculated from the video's actual `publishedAt` timestamp to snapshot time, never from `views ÷ vph`.**
- The **Outlier multiple** column is this series' own calculation: a video's vidIQ `vph` divided by the median `vph` of that channel's 10 immediately preceding tracked uploads (excluding the video itself). It is **not** a vidIQ field.
- All five rows below are flagged **"fresh"** — every one is under 25 hours old at snapshot time, so these multiples lean on vph reads that are still inflated by recency and will likely come down as each video matures.
- AICodeKing's newest upload ("Claude's OFFICIAL ENGINEER Mode", ~25.0x its own baseline, breakoutScore still null at ~18h old) narrowly missed the table below — its multiple is close to Theo's but its absolute reach (8.4K views) is far smaller.
