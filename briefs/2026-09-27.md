## Takeaway for creators in this niche
A strong `breakoutScore` on day one is not proof a title template works — today's data shows one confirmed 1.56 breakout collapsing to 0.88 by the next day while a modest 2.56 elsewhere quietly grew to 6.94. If you're optimizing for a format that keeps paying off rather than a one-day spike, favor titles that lead with a specific, checkable result (a real number, a real outcome) over vague-hype or subject-withholding hooks, and don't declare a pattern "repeatable" off a single early read — check it again after 24-48 hours.
