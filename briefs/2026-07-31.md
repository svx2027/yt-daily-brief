**3. ## Takeaway for creators

Naming a specific, newer, or under-covered AI coding tool directly in the title — paired with a short (5–10 min) build-along demo — is the most consistently repeating overperformance template in this dataset this week (seen independently across at least four channels: MiniMax, OmniRoute+OpenCode, Ollama+OpenCode, SuperCode CLI). The window for any one tool name is likely short before search saturates; the actionable move is to scan for tools that just shipped or updated and publish a title-specific demo fast, rather than another generic "AI coding tips" video.
