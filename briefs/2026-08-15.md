## Takeaway for creators

Today's biggest breakout (Fireship, ~13.1x) didn't come from AI-tool coverage at all — it came from a channel using its established investigative format on an adjacent tech-policy story, a reminder that a channel's *format* can be the real breakout asset, not just its niche.
