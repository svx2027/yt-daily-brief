## Takeaway for creators

Every top performer here names something specific rather than talking about "AI" in general: a named tool (Claude, not "an AI assistant"), a named person (Karpathy, not "an expert"), or a named capability/weakness (motion graphics, "biggest weakness," not "new features"). The channels that won this week didn't invent new formats — they pointed an already-proven title template (head-to-head matchup, "[tool] just unlocked X," borrowed-authority) at Claude-specific content instead of generic AI coverage. For creators in this niche, the actionable move is auditing your own back catalog for whichever title template already has the best breakout score, then re-running it against whatever shipped in the AI-coding space this week — rather than reaching for a new angle each time.
