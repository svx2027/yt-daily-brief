## Takeaway for creators in this niche

When the news itself is already the draw — a real, developing incident or a just-released, name-recognized tool — naming the subject directly in the title can outperform a withholding/curiosity-gap template, even on a channel that normally withholds it. The withholding trick works when *you* are the reason to click; naming it works when the subject already is. Match the title mechanic to which one is true for that specific story before defaulting to either house style.
